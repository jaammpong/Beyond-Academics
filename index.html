Case Study: Balancing Hobbies and Academic Responsibilities

Introduction

Everyone has different hobbies that make their free time more enjoyable. For me, playing online games and cooking are two activities that I really love doing. Games like Valorant, Counter-Strike 2, Dota 2, Tekken, and Mobile Legends give me entertainment, while cooking allows me to prepare something for my family. However, having hobbies also means learning how to manage my time, especially when I have school activities and other responsibilities.

Main Case

As a student, I sometimes experience stress because of academic activities, assignments, and other school responsibilities. Whenever I have free time, I usually enjoy playing online games. I like competitive games because they challenge my skills, patience, teamwork, and decision-making. Every game has its own difficulty, and that's one of the reasons why I find them interesting.

However, I also understand that spending too much time playing games can affect my studies and daily routine. Sometimes, it's hard to stop playing, especially when I'm enjoying the match or playing with friends. But I try to remind myself that gaming is only a hobby and should not become my main priority.

The Challenges I Experience

One of the challenges I experience is managing my time between entertainment and responsibilities. There are times when I want to play more games, but I still have schoolwork to finish. Dili man always sayon ang pag-manage sa time, especially when you're enjoying what you're doing.

Another challenge is maintaining my interest in other activities. Aside from gaming, I also love cooking, especially for my family. I'm not a professional cook, but I enjoy preparing different dishes whenever I have the opportunity. Sometimes, I just experiment with the ingredients available at home and see what I can make. It's a simple hobby, but seeing my family enjoy the food makes me happy.

How I Manage My Time

To avoid spending too much time on gaming, I try to limit my playing schedule. Usually, I only play once or twice a week, depending on my free time and school workload. I also make sure that my school responsibilities come first before entertainment.

For cooking, I usually do it whenever I have extra time at home. I don't follow a strict schedule because I only cook when I feel like preparing something for my family. These activities help me enjoy my free time without completely forgetting my responsibilities.

Lessons I Learned

Through these experiences, I realized that having hobbies is important because they allow us to enjoy life outside academics. Gaming helps me develop patience, strategy, and teamwork, while cooking allows me to become more creative and appreciate the simple things I can do for my family.

I cannot say that I'm already perfect at managing my time because sometimes I still struggle with it. But I'm trying to improve little by little. I learned that enjoying your hobbies doesn't mean you have to spend all your time doing them.

Conclusion

In the end, I believe that gaming and cooking are more than just activities I do during my free time. They are things that make me happy and help me relax. I don't need to give up my hobbies just because I have academic responsibilities. What matters is knowing my priorities and understanding when to take a break. As a student, I'm still learning how to balance everything, and I think that's part of growing and becoming more responsible.
